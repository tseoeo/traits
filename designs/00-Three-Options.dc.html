traits by lozeva — site explorations

Three ways to show every photograph

traits by lozeva is a working portrait studio in Sofia, standing on a 1,412-portrait archive (2016–2026). All three options display that whole archive on one surface — they differ in what the mass means: a museum wall, a single current, or a strip of film. Each keeps the three real actions findable: Book a session, Gift vouchers, and Corporate & editorial enquiries — present, never pushy. Shared across all three: equal treatment of every sitter, near-black or paper grounds, a БГ/EN toggle that carries between pages, decade-proof structure, and prefers-reduced-motion respected. All names, venues, dates, and the dataset of 1,412 records are invented placeholders, and the action links (#book / #vouchers) are entry-point stubs to wire to real booking and voucher checkout.

1,412 portraits. ONE WALL · ONE ORDER

The Wall — kept

gallery at night

Thesis: the photographer is an institution, and the proof is visual mass. The page is the archive — a charcoal contact-sheet wall of all 1,412 frames, banded by year, faces emerging like silver-gelatin prints. Architectural, disciplined, chronological.

  • Structure: rigid uniform grid, year bands, vertical scroll
  • Ground: gallery-at-night charcoal; hover brightens, never scales
  • Business: a Book a session button in the nav; a Corporate view of provenance-captioned work; vouchers in About

The Stream — new

one current

Thesis: remove the museum entirely. No grid, no year bands, no imposed order — the 1,412 portraits run together as a single justified current on warm paper, at reading size. Where the Wall is architecture, the Stream is humanist: the faces make the mass, not the scaffolding around them.

  • Structure: continuous masonry flow, varied crops, no banding
  • Ground: warm paper, faces large; rest reveals the name
  • Business: a colophon footer — book, vouchers, corporate — set in the same quiet serif as the bio

Honesty note: dropping the year bands trades legibility of when for pure flow; varied crops mean accepting non-uniform framing (the opposite of the Wall's typology), and faces at reading size make for a long scroll and heavy image load.

The Reel — new

a strip of film

Thesis: the archive as one continuous film strip. Navigation turns sideways — you scrub horizontally through ten years while a giant year ticks over as you travel. The sheer length of the roll is the body of work made physical: this strip runs on and on.

  • Structure: single horizontal strip, chronological, frame-numbered
  • Ground: lightbox black; a year scrubs as you drag or scroll
  • Business: a Book a session chip in the header; book / vouchers / corporate in the end footer

Honesty note: horizontal scroll fights web habits (mitigated: wheel maps to sideways, drag-to-pan) and shows few faces at once — it trades the Wall's instant all-at-once gestalt for a slower, temporal reveal. The length that's the point also makes it slow to traverse end to end.
