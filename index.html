About

Lozeva (b. 1971, Sofia) has photographed in black and white for the past ten years. Her portraits — politicians, writers, actors, scientists, and people otherwise unrecorded — are made in available light, on the same terms, with the same patience. Since 2016 she has kept a single continuous archive; it now holds more than fourteen hundred portraits. traits by lozeva is her working studio in Sofia.
